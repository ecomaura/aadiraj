Our Story

Curating taste, one plate at a time

Aadiraj began in 2026 with a simple idea: a neighbourhood snack stop where the food tastes like it was made for you, because it was.

We pick our ingredients carefully, cook in small batches and keep the menu honest. Come by for a quick bite on your way home, a tea break with friends, or something cold on a warm Thane afternoon.
